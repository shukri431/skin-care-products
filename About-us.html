  • Rooted in Nature

    Made in Canada

    Perfect for autumn Skin

    Nestled within the 500 acres of our property lies Ste. Anne’s Apothecary, a haven of mindful
    craftsmanship. Here, the surroundings and the flourishing Ste. Anne’s gardens serve as
    wellsprings of inspiration. Each item we create is a testament to the beauty that nature
    graciously provides.

    Depending on the season, the team gathers flowers and herbs and through an ancient
    distillation process produces extracts for soap, bath goods, balms, floral mists and butters. We
    source most ingredients directly from our gardens or from sustainable and ethical suppliers.

    The Story of
    skinco Apothecary

    The Apothecary traces back to the vision of
    one of our gardeners, Darlene, who sadly
    passed away last year. Her legacy lives on
    through the passionate efforts of her
    granddaughter, Madison O’Connor. The
    narrative of Ste. Anne’s Apothecary is woven
    with threads of love, resilience and familial
    bonds. Darlene, alongside her friend Debbie,
    started the Ste. Anne’s gardens over two
    decades ago. Over time, Madison stands as a
    testament to the enduring spirit of her
    grandmother’s vision, nurturing it into a
    vibrant reality.
